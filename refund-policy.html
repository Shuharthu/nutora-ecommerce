Return & Refund Policy

1. Food Product Safety Standard

Because NUTORA prepares artisanal food items without artificial chemical preservatives, opened or consumed jars cannot be returned for health and sanitation reasons.

2. Transit Damage & Defect Replacements

If your parcel arrives with a broken glass jar, damaged safety seal, or defective product, please notify us within 24 hours of receiving the package with a quick photo or unboxing video sent to our WhatsApp (+880 1712-345678).

3. Instant Resolution Options

  • Free Immediate Replacement: We dispatch a fresh replacement jar with priority delivery.
  • Full Refund: If replacement is not desired, we issue a full refund to your bKash, Nagad, or bank account within 3–5 business days.

4. Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled before dispatch without any penalty by calling our kitchen helpline at +880 1712-345678.
