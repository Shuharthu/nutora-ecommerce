Kitchen Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our ingredients, delivery, and storage.

Are NUTORA products really 100% homemade?
Yes! All our offerings are handcrafted in small, hygienic home batches right here in Dhaka following authentic maternal heritage recipes. We never use factory stabilizers, palm oil, or artificial preservatives.
Why does oil separate on top of the peanut butter?
Because our peanut butter contains zero hydrogenated oils or chemical emulsifiers! Natural peanut oil rises naturally to the top. Simply give it a good stir with a dry spoon before enjoying.
How is your raw honey harvested?
Our wildflower honey is responsibly harvested from the pristine mangrove forests of the Sundarbans by indigenous Mauals. It is unpasteurized, unheated, and unfiltered through micro-pore strainers, retaining natural bee pollen and enzymes.
What are your delivery charges across Bangladesh?
Inside Dhaka Metropolitan: ৳70 (Delivery in 24–48 hours). Free delivery on orders over ৳1,500!
Outside Dhaka (all 64 districts): ৳130 (Delivery in 3–5 days via secure courier).
Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available?
Yes! Cash on Delivery is fully supported for all addresses in Bangladesh. You inspect the parcel and pay directly to the delivery person.
What if my glass jar breaks during transit?
We cushion every glass jar with multiple shock-absorbing layers. In the rare event of transit damage, take a photo upon receipt and message our WhatsApp (+880 1712-345678). We will immediately dispatch a fresh replacement jar at zero additional cost!
