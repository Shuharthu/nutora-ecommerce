Shipping & Delivery Policy

1. Service Coverage Across Bangladesh

NUTORA delivers across all 64 districts in Bangladesh. All food products are packed in sanitary, tamper-evident glass jars or protective earthenware pots cushioned against transit vibration.

2. Delivery Charges & Timelines

Zone Delivery Fee Estimated Time
Dhaka City ৳70 (FREE over ৳1,500) 24 – 48 Hours
Outside Dhaka (Nationwide) ৳130 3 – 5 Business Days

3. Cash on Delivery (COD)

We accept Cash on Delivery for all destinations. Please keep the exact amount ready upon delivery.

4. Packaging & Glass Safety Guarantee

Every glass jar is individually shrink-banded and bubble-wrapped in recyclable corrugated shippers. In case of any transit breakage, we replace the item free of charge.
